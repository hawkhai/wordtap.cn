Charis Regular 7.000, unmodified WOFF2 web font.
Source: https://software.sil.org/downloads/r/charis/Charis-7.000.zip
Project: https://software.sil.org/charis/
Character coverage: https://software.sil.org/charis/charset/
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see OFL.txt for copyright and terms.

Self-hosted for WordTap phonetic reading via --wordtap-phonetic-font-family.
Includes the complete IPA Extensions, Spacing Modifier Letters, Phonetic
Extensions and Phonetic Extensions Supplement blocks, plus the additional
letters, combining diacritics, ties and tone symbols on the standard IPA chart.
Regular weight only; no synthetic bold or italic.
